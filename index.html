Share from other apps (iOS Shortcut)

Make a Shortcut so you can send text here from the share sheet:

  1. Open Shortcuts, tap +, and name it RSVP Read.
  2. Tap the ⓘ button and turn on Show in Share Sheet. Set it to receive Text, Rich Text, PDFs, Safari web pages and URLs. Under “If there’s no input”, choose Get Clipboard.
  3. Add the action Get Text from Input (input: Shortcut Input).
  4. Add Copy to Clipboard (input: Text).
  5. Add Open URLs with:

Then pick RSVP Read from any app’s share sheet and tap “Tap to paste & read”. To share a web page as an article, put Get Article using Safari Reader then Get Details of Article → Body before step 3. From Google Docs, select all and copy, or use Share & export → Send a copy → PDF and choose RSVP Read.

Keys on a computer: Space = play/pause, ←/→ = word, ⇧←/⇧→ = sentence, ↑/↓ = speed, Esc = close.
